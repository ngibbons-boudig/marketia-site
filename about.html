about

the one behind the camera.

i’m tia, the founder of marketia. i capture the moments, details and stories behind businesses, brands and creators, mostly on an iphone, always with a creative lens.

the story

how marketia came to be.

i’ve always been the friend who documents absolutely everything. the one making everyone redo their cheers for the perfect shot, forever running out of storage at dinner, and getting chased for the photos the morning after.

i lived in sydney for two years, and that’s where i truly fell in love with content creation and decided to make a career of it. at the start of 2026 i moved back home to dorset to pursue all things socials and content.

since then i’ve had the chance to work with some incredible brands, businesses and creators, from ongoing social media management to one‑off content shoots, helping bring their ideas to life.

marketia is a little space of my own to bring it all together: the work i create, the people i work with, and everything that goes on behind the scenes.

the approach

natural, not staged.

i’d rather capture what’s really happening than build a set around it. it’s what makes content feel current, and it’s what people actually stop for.
