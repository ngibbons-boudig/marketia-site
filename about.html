the story

how marketia came to be.

I’ve always been the friend who documents absolutely everything. The one making everyone redo their cheers for the perfect shot, forever running out of storage at dinner and getting chased for the photos the morning after. I’ve always loved capturing the little details, the atmosphere and the moments you want to remember.

Living in Sydney for two years was a huge part of my journey. It’s where I really fell in love with content creation, made friends for life working in the creative industry and found myself surrounded by people who continue to inspire, motivate and support me, even from the other side of the world. I also started sharing more of my own life online, documenting my Australian adventure and growing my personal brand along the way. It made me realise that something I’d always loved could become something much more.

In December 2025, I moved back home to Dorset with a new perspective and a real desire to turn that passion into a career of my own. Since then, I’ve had the chance to work with some incredible local businesses, creators and brands. Whether it’s ongoing social media management, a one‑off content shoot or bringing a campaign to life, no two projects are quite the same.

Marketia is my own creative brand and a place to bring all of my work together. A little look at the brands I work with, the content I create, the projects I’m part of and everything that happens behind the scenes. It’s where I share what I do, how I do it and the people I get to do it with.
