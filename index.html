meet the founder

TIA

i've always been the friend who documents absolutely everything. the one making everyone redo their cheers three times for the perfect shot, forever running out of storage at dinner, and getting chased for the photos the morning after.

after two years living in sydney, where i truly fell in love with content creation, i moved back home to dorset at the start of 2026 to pursue all things socials & content. since then i've worked with some incredible brands, businesses and creators, from ongoing social media management to one‑off content shoots, helping bring their ideas to life.

marketia is a little space of my own to bring it all together: the work i create, the people i work with, and everything that goes on behind the scenes.
