how it works

from hello to handover.

Every collaboration starts with understanding your business, what you’re looking for and how I can help. From there, we’ll put a plan together that works for you and your brand.

let’s talk

Send over an enquiry with a little about you and/or your business, what you have in mind and the support you’re looking for. We’ll arrange a call to talk through your ideas, goals and what you’d like to achieve.

the plan

Once I’ve got a feel for what you’re looking for, I’ll put together a tailored proposal outlining the approach, services, pricing and creative direction. We’ll agree on the details, confirm everything and get things underway.

let’s get creating

For ongoing management, we’ll work through onboarding, content planning, brand direction and the upcoming content calendar before getting started. For content days, we’ll agree on the shoot plan, timings and everything to be captured before the day itself. From there, I’ll take care of the content creation, editing and delivery, or manage your socials from planning through to publishing.

pricing

Pricing is tailored to each business and what it needs. Management packages are based on the platforms, posting frequency and volume of content; shoot days are priced on the hours needed and the amount of content to be produced. Send an enquiry and I’ll put a simple quote together.
