how it works

simple, from hello to handover.

no long proposals. a quick conversation about what you need, a plan, and then the fun part.

say hello

send an enquiry with a little about your business and what you’re after. i’ll come back to you with thoughts and availability.

plan the day

we agree the shape of it together: the story to tell, what to capture, where and when, and what you’ll walk away with.

capture & handover

i come in and capture it, then edit and deliver content you can post straight away, or run it for you month to month.

pricing

pricing is tailored to each business and what it needs. management packages are based on the platforms, posting frequency and volume of content; shoot days are priced on the hours needed and the amount of content to be produced. send an enquiry and i’ll put a simple quote together.
